AI OR AUTOMATION? COMPARISON GUIDE

Does Your Business Actually Need AI? (AI vs. Automation Explained)

Every technology company is pitching "AI" today. But not every business problem needs complex AI—sometimes simple automation is cheaper, faster, and better.

The Simple Difference:

Scenario 1: Sending Invoices

"Send invoice PDF automatically every Friday."
👉 This is Automation. It follows a fixed rule.

Scenario 2: Reading Bills

"Read an incoming vendor bill and figure out invoice number & tax amount."
👉 This is AI. It requires reading and understanding context.

Scenario 3: Moving Customer Data

"Copy customer contact details from a form into a database."
👉 This is Automation. It follows structured steps.

4. Replying to Open Customer Questions

"Understand what a customer is asking and decide which answer they need."
👉 This is AI. It involves natural human conversation.
