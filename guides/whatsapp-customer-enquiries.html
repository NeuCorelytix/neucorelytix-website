WHATSAPP CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION GUIDE

Tired of Answering the Same WhatsApp Questions Every Day?

In India, WhatsApp is where customer conversations happen. Here is how growing businesses handle routine WhatsApp inquiries 24/7 without taking team time.

For most retail stores, wholesale distributors, and service providers in India, WhatsApp brings in leads. But staff end up typing the exact same answers dozens of times a day: "What is the price?", "Is this in stock?", or "Send location."

Step 1: Identify Your Top 3 Repeated WhatsApp Questions

Track your business WhatsApp chat log for 3 days. You will find that 80% of customer messages belong to 3 routine buckets:

1. Price List & Catalog Inquiries

Customer: "Send price list for Category X."
Automated Answer: Sends PDF catalog or price table instantly.

2. Stock Availability & Timing

Customer: "Is Size M available today?"
Automated Answer: Checks stock database and confirms availability immediately.

3. Order Status & Tracking

Customer: "Where is my order #1042?"
Automated Answer: Fetches live delivery status and sends tracking link.

What Should You NOT Automate on WhatsApp?

  • Custom Price Negotiations: Large wholesale orders require human negotiation.
  • Urgent Customer Complaints: Upset customers need immediate human empathy.
