STOCK PLANNER PURCHASING DECISION GUIDE

How Much Stock Should I Buy Next Month?

Buy too much → cash gets trapped in unsold inventory sitting on shelves. Buy too little → you lose sales when customers walk away empty-handed.

Most business purchasing decisions happen by gut feel: "Product A sold well last month, let's reorder 500 units." But buying patterns shift dynamically.

How to Calculate Your Reorder Point (ROP) Manually

To avoid stockouts, calculate your Reorder Point for key products using this formula:

Reorder Point = (Daily Sales × Supplier Lead Days) + Safety Buffer

Example: If you sell 10 boxes per day and your supplier takes 5 days to deliver, your base requirement is 50 boxes. Adding a 10-box safety buffer means you must reorder whenever stock hits 60 boxes.
