How Do I Know Which Products Are Selling the Most in My Shop?

Revenue tells you how much money came in. Business intelligence tells you which specific items, brands, and categories actually drove that revenue.

At the end of the month, your bank statement or UPI settlement tells you your revenue: say, ₹8,40,000.

That number feels great, but it doesn't answer the core questions needed to grow: Which products are accelerating? Which items look busy but barely make profit? What should we reorder next month?

How to Calculate Product Sales Velocity Manually

High price tags don't equal high importance. Calculate Sales Velocity in Excel or your sales register using this formula:

Sales Velocity (Units/Day) = Total Units Sold ÷ Days in Period

Item A (High Margin, Low Velocity)

1 unit sold per week @ ₹1,000 margin
Velocity: 0.14 units/day (₹1,000/week)

Item B (Moderate Margin, High Velocity)

15 units sold per day @ ₹100 margin
Velocity: 15 units/day (₹10,500/week)

3 Key Signals to Watch in Your Sales Data

  • Weekend Spikes: Items that sell 2.5x faster on Saturdays require Friday reordering.
  • Category Acceleration: A sub-category growing 3 months in a row signals shifting customer tastes.
  • Slow Shelf Turnover: Items taking longer than 60 days to flip tie up valuable working capital.
