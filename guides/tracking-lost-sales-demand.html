LOST DEMAND CUSTOMER INSIGHT GUIDE

The Products Customers Asked For—but You Never Sold

Your billing software tells you what customers bought. It doesn't tell you what they asked for and walked away without buying.

Imagine a customer messages your business on WhatsApp: "Do you have Size 42 in Black?". Your staff replies: "Sorry, out of stock." The customer leaves.

Another customer asks for a specific brand of hardware or a custom fabric color. You don't carry it. They leave. None of these interactions appear in your POS, billing software, or end-of-month sales report because no financial transaction took place.

Why Sales Data Has a Massive Blindspot

Traditional business reports only record successful transactions. They tell you:

  • Item A sold 40 units
  • Item B sold 12 units

What they don't tell you is that 47 customers asked for Item C over the past 30 days. Stocking Item C would yield guaranteed sales on day one, yet it remains completely invisible in standard business software.

How to Start Tracking Lost Demand Manually (Free Excel Template)

When Manual Spreadsheet Logging Breaks Down

A manual log works great when you get 5 inquiries a day. But as your business grows, sales staff get busy during peak hours, forget to make entries, or reply to WhatsApp chats without logging missed items.
