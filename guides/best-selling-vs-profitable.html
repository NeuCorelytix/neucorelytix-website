Your Best-Selling Product Might Not Be Your Most Profitable Product

High revenue numbers can deceive you. See why looking at profit margins and product attachments completely changes your purchasing decisions.

Suppose your end-of-month sales records show:

  • Product A: ₹2,00,000 in total sales
  • Product B: ₹80,000 in total sales

Most business owners naturally assume Product A is far more valuable. But let's calculate net profit:

Product A (High Volume)

₹2,00,000 Sales
8% Margin = ₹16,000 Net Profit

Product B (High Margin)

₹80,000 Sales
35% Margin = ₹28,000 Net Profit

How to Calculate Real Product Margin Manually

Net Margin % = [(Selling Price - Supplier Cost - Freight) ÷ Selling Price] × 100

Furthermore, customers who buy Product B frequently buy high-margin accessories along with it. Product B is actually driving your entire business profit, while Product A simply creates high turnover with low margin.
